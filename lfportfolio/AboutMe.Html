About Me

Lavender Fong

" Hello! My name is Lavender. I am a queer animator based in Columbia, Missouri. I graduated from the University of Missouri in May 2026, with a bachelors in Film production, and three minors: theatre, digital storytelling, and Sociology. I specialize in 2D and 3D animation. Something I want to work on is my storyboarding, and Blender Skills.

Some fun facts about me is, I programmed this website. I love learning programming languages, as well as actual spoken language. I am currently learning and improving my Spanish, French, and Cantonese. I also like cooking. I have so many memories of being in the kitchen as a little kid with my Father. I just enjoy making food, food is just good. My favorite food currently is rice cakes or tacos."
